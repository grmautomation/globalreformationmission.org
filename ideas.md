**Design Movement:** Liturgical Editorial Modernism with cartographic mission cues.

**Core Principles:** First, every visual decision must communicate theological gravity without becoming visually heavy. Second, content should move from conviction to invitation, using scripture, mission language, and clear next steps as the primary hierarchy. Third, ornament must serve comprehension: rules, compass lines, and scripture panels should guide the eye rather than decorate. Fourth, the website should feel local and global at once, pairing warm church hospitality with disciplined missionary scope.

**Color Philosophy:** Reformation Navy and Midnight Mission form the authoritative ground, suggesting doctrine, depth, and steadiness. Parchment White creates hospitable reading surfaces that reduce fatigue and evoke printed theological material. Covenant Gold is used sparingly as a sacred rule, chapter marker, or emphasis color, while Mission Blue is reserved for clear action and discoverability.

**Layout Paradigm:** An editorial field layout with asymmetric scripture panels, mission ledgers, offset visual cards, and sectional chapter markers. Rather than centering every block, each page should feel like a carefully composed missionary report, with a strong left rail for labels and a broader right field for narrative and action.

**Signature Elements:** Thin covenant-gold rules, faint global map coordinates, and scripture/reference cards framed like formal liturgical readings.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interactions should be restrained, confident, and purposeful. Hover states should reveal clarity rather than spectacle: a blue action glow, a gold rule extension, or a subtle card lift.

**Animation:** Use slow editorial reveals, line-drawing transitions, and gentle parallax on map textures. Avoid bouncy motion. Section entrances should feel like a printed page being opened: opacity, slight vertical lift, and measured timing.

**Typography System:** Cormorant Garamond for display statements, H1s, theological pull quotes, and major section openers. Source Sans 3 for body, UI labels, navigation, and cards. Labels should use Source Sans 3 SemiBold in small caps with wide tracking. Hierarchy should contrast large serif convictions with compact sans-serif practical details.

## Chosen Direction

The website will commit to **Liturgical Editorial Modernism with cartographic mission cues**. This is the closest extension of the existing style guide and best supports a complete mission website: doctrinal conviction, local welcome, global sending, teaching clarity, and streaming access. Every page and component should ask: **Does this choice reinforce or dilute our design philosophy?**
