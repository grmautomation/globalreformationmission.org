# Global Reformation Mission Website Audit Findings

## Brand style guide observations

The existing style guide positions Global Reformation Mission as **“Reformed in conviction. Missionary in movement.”** The stated identity is a disciplined web identity emphasizing biblical authority, local hospitality, global church-planting vision, and clear pathways for worship, partnership, and giving.

The style guide defines the mission positioning as: **Global Reformation Mission exists to plant and strengthen Bible-centered churches that equip the saints, reach the lost, and transform communities for the glory of God.** The named design movement is **Liturgical Editorial Modernism**, described as formal, spacious, scripture-centered, restrained, and usable across local church and nonprofit mission contexts.

Brand colors identified:

| Role | Color | Usage |
|---|---:|---|
| Reformation Navy | `#031B3D` | Primary authority background |
| Midnight Mission | `#06112A` | Deep section panels |
| Parchment White | `#F7F3E8` | Warm reading surfaces |
| Covenant Gold | `#E5B84B` | Sacred emphasis and rules |
| Mission Blue | `#1E9BFF` | Links, buttons, focus states |
| Steel Blue | `#7DA9C7` | Borders, map lines, secondary details |

Typography identified:

| Role | Typeface | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Cormorant Garamond | H1, theological statements, section openers |
| Body | Source Sans 3 | Paragraphs, cards, navigation, forms |
| Labels | Source Sans 3 SemiBold | Small caps, scripture references, UI metadata |

Homepage pattern recommended by the style guide: hero with mission, worship time, location, and primary action; mission pillars for Word, Grace, Community, Mission, Glory; visit section; church planting section; partner pathways including pray, give, go, and send. The guide emphasizes restrained ornament, clear hierarchy, strong contrast, and repeated next steps.
